Adds `Switcher`: children in one row of equal widths that all stack at once when
any would be narrower than `min` (`s`, `m` or `l`). It loads with `styles.css`,
or on its own as `switcher.css`. It needs `sibling-count()`: Chrome 138, Safari
26.2 and Firefox 154.
